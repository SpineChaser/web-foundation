About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool that helps users write, organize, and keep track of important notes. It is designed to make recording ideas, reminders, and everyday tasks easy.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. Write down your ideas, reminders, or tasks.
  3. Organize your notes so they are easy to find.
  4. Review your notes whenever you need them.

Features

  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • Quick recording of ideas and reminders.
  • Organized notes for everyday tasks.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for faster navigation.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + S Save your work in supported applications.
Ctrl + N Create a new document in supported applications.
Ctrl + F Find text on a page.
